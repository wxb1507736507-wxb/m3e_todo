feat: give every todo its own reminder, ringing or quiet, with its own ringtone

The reminder was app-wide: one switch for "ring or just a message" and one
ringtone, applied to every dated todo. Urgency is per task — the deadline that
matters should ring and the shopping list should not — so both the mode and the
sound now live on the todo. "Follow the app setting" is the third state and the
default, so a todo that was never given a choice keeps tracking the default and
older files load unchanged (todos.json v4).

Android gives a notification channel its sound and forbids changing it
afterwards, which is why the old scheme minted a versioned channel per ringtone
change and deleted the previous one: six channels of debris on the test device,
and the user's own per-channel settings thrown away each time. The native side
now keeps one channel per distinct sound, created on demand, named after the
ringtone so it is identifiable in Android's settings, and reused by every todo
that picks it; the legacy channels are deleted on first launch. Reminders also
no longer assume Android 8+ — before channels exist the sound goes on the
notification itself.

The settings sheet keeps the same two controls, now labelled as the default, and
says so.
